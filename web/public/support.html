Support

Help with Tablewhere on iPhone, iMessage, and the web vote page.

Contact: email support@glofly.app and include your iPhone model and what you were doing. We usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

Why don’t I see a restaurant I know is nearby?

Tablewhere uses Apple Maps. Pull down on Discover to search again, try a bigger radius, or check that “No fast food” and cuisine filters are off. Not on Apple Maps? Add it yourself from the Lists tab with +.

How do friends join a group plan without the app?

Share the link from the group screen (or send the web link from iMessage). They open it in any browser, enter a first name, and vote or add their pick.

My lists aren’t on my other iPhone or iPad.

Lists sync through iCloud. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive is on for both devices.

I’m not getting “time to leave” reminders.

Open Tablewhere → Settings → Notifications and turn alerts on (or allow them in iPhone Settings → Tablewhere → Notifications).

How do I stop sharing anonymous usage counts?

Tablewhere → Settings → turn off “Share anonymous usage counts.”

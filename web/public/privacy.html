Tablewhere

Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

The short version: Tablewhere doesn’t show ads, doesn’t track you across apps or websites, and doesn’t sell anything about you. Your location stays on your phone. Group plans share only what the group needs: the first name you choose and the places and votes you add.

Your location

Tablewhere uses your location while you’re using the app to find restaurants near you and to work out when you should leave for a group plan. Searches go to Apple Maps under Apple’s privacy policy. Your location is not sent to Tablewhere’s servers or shared with your group. You can pick a city instead, or turn location off in iPhone Settings.

Your lists

Favorites, places you want to try, places you’ve blacklisted, notes, areas, home recipes, and spin history are stored on your phone and synced through your own iCloud account if iCloud is on. Tablewhere can’t see them.

Group plans

When you start or join a group (in the app, iMessage, or on the web vote page), Tablewhere stores this in Google Firebase so everyone in the group can see it:

  • the first name you choose, and a random anonymous ID (no account, email, or phone number)
  • the places you add, your votes, and, for the person who started it, the meeting time and note
  • a scrambled form of your blacklisted places, so the group can avoid them without seeing your list
  • if you allow notifications, a device token so the group can alert you

Anyone with a group’s link or code can see that group. When its creator ends a group, its votes, members, and details are deleted.

Usage counts

Tablewhere counts anonymous daily totals, like how many spins or groups happened today, to understand how the app is used. These are only numbers; they include no names, places, locations, or IDs and can’t be linked to you. Turn this off in Tablewhere → Settings → “Share anonymous usage counts.”

Calendar and notifications

If you tap Add to Calendar, Tablewhere adds that one event; it can’t read your calendar. Reminders like “Time to head out” are scheduled on your phone.

The web vote page

The vote page stores the name you enter in your browser so you don’t have to type it again. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and uses Apple Maps search, so those services receive standard web request information such as your IP address.

Services Tablewhere uses

  • Apple: Maps and MapKit (restaurant search and maps), iCloud (your lists)
  • Google Firebase: anonymous sign-in, group data, usage counts, web hosting

Children

Tablewhere isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. For questions, visit forkroad-app.web.app/support.html.
